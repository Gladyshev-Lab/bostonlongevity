About the Hub

Boston Longevity Hub connects the community working on aging, longevity, and age-related diseases. Boston brings together an exceptional concentration of academic laboratories, hospitals, biotechnology companies, investors, foundations, and other organizations advancing research and translation in this field.

A major annual activity is Boston Longevity Week, which brings together independently organized conferences, symposia, workshops, and other events. Each event is run by its own organizers; the Hub lists them side by side so that the week can be seen as a whole.

Our broader goal is to strengthen connections across the Boston longevity community and make its people, organizations, research, and activities more visible and accessible year-round.

The Hub is run by people who study aging in the Greater Boston area and organize meetings in the field. If something here is missing or out of date, let us know.
